## The sentence

> The best meal I ever had was pho eaten standing at a Hanoi curb at 2 a.m., the broth so hot it fogged my glasses, and I didn't care.

## Design note

What carries the sentence is a single remembered moment told with concrete particulars: the dish is named, the place and hour are fixed, and one bodily detail — steam fogging the writer's glasses — does the work of proving the memory is lived rather than invented. The closing shrug is the turn that stays with the reader: comfort lost out to the meal, and the writer would make the same trade again.
